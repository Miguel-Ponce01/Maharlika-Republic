# Maharlika Republic - Vision and Scope

## 1. Project Vision (Task 1.1)
Maharlika Republic (Marexx Republic) aims to be Davao City's premier premium Apple devices digital catalog and physical showroom platform. It bridges the gap between digital convenience and physical store trust by offering a curated online catalog, visual flexible financing visualizers (Paluwagan, Lay-away, Skyro, Salmon), a physical showroom locator with interactive indoor layout mapping, and a seamless Messenger-based transaction checkout system.

## 2. Project Scope (Task 1.1)
- **In Scope:**
    - Interactive premium Apple device catalog (iPhone, iPad, Mac, accessories).
    - Map-based location visualizer with dual-canvas mapping (Macro city view & Micro showroom indoor layout using MapLibre GL).
    - Zustand state-managed persistent shopping cart and drawer.
    - Integrated checkout layout and digital invoice generation.
    - Copy-to-clipboard transaction payload for quick Facebook Messenger dispatch.
    - Visualizing financing plans (Paluwagan, Lay-Away, Skyro, Salmon) via interactive Instagram-style story highlights.
    - Full-scale adaptive dark and light theme toggling.
- **Out of Scope:**
    - Live automated merchant payment settlement (handled either via manual showroom terminal swipe, cash-on-delivery, or offline bank/GCash/Maya transfers).
    - Automatic shipping courier carrier integrations (limited to local cash-on-delivery shipping and showroom pickup).

## 3. Target Users (Task 1.2)
- **Tech-Savvy Davao City Buyers:** Seeking authentic, rigorously checked pre-owned and brand-new Apple devices.
- **Budget-Conscious Consumers:** Seeking to acquire premium gadgets through monthly contributions (Paluwagan, 0% Lay-away) or local digital financing partners (Skyro, Salmon, GGives).
- **Physical Inspection Shoppers:** Consumers who value seeing the physical device in the Bajada showroom before finalizing their purchase.

## 4. Market Research & Core Problems (Tasks 1.3 & 1.4)
- **Facebook Marketplace / General Groups:** Crowded with unverified listings, fake accounts, duplicate spam, and a complete lack of battery health/physical condition checks.
- **Traditional Retail Stores:** High upfront costs with rigid payment options, lacking easy layout visualization for the physical store.
- **Core Problems Identified:**
    - Lack of trust and authentic condition verification.
    - Lack of clear visual guidance for store locations and indoor layouts.
    - Complexity and lack of clear transparency in local flexible financing options.

## 5. Unique Value Proposition (UVP) (Task 1.5)
Maharlika Republic offers a **verified, flexible, and visually interactive** Apple device acquisition experience.
- **Showroom Mapping:** Dual-canvas MapLibre GL implementation lets users inspect our showroom layout before stepping foot in Bajada.
- **Transparent Financing:** Visual, Instagram-style story cards explain our lay-away and local financing plans instantly.
- **Messenger Dispatch Checkouts:** A dynamic checkout generator constructs the cart context into a single-click copy-paste payload for quick sales representative verification.

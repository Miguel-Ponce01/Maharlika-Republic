# KomuniTrade - Vision and Scope

## 1. Project Vision (Task 1.1)
KomuniTrade aims to be the leading hyperlocal marketplace system that empowers local communities (students, households, small-scale sellers) to trade safely and efficiently. By leveraging AI (CNN and OCR) and strict verification methods, we solve the trust and safety issues prevalent in existing platforms.

## 2. Project Scope (Task 1.1)
- **In Scope:**
    - Hyperlocal listing and search (Geohash).
    - AI-assisted listing generation (image classification, text extraction).
    - Seller verification (facial recognition) and credibility scoring.
    - Anonymous real-time chat.
    - Automated listing expiration (TTL).
- **Out of Scope:**
    - Payment processing (transactions are handled offline or via external methods by users).
    - Delivery logistics (users arrange meeting points).

## 3. Target Users (Task 1.2)
- **Students:** Looking for affordable items, textbooks, and easy selling within campus or dorm areas.
- **Households:** Selling unused items or buying from neighbors.
- **Small-Scale Sellers:** Local artisans, home bakers, and small businesses needing a platform to reach local customers without high fees.

## 4. Market Research & Core Problems (Tasks 1.3 & 1.4)
- **Facebook Marketplace:** High volume but full of scams, lack of verification, and difficult to filter by exact location relevance.
- **Carousell / OLX:** Better structure but still faces trust issues and lacks the automated, AI-driven listing features that make posting frictionless for casual sellers.
- **Core Problems Identified:**
    - Lack of trust and seller verification.
    - Difficulty in finding truly local (hyperlocal) items.
    - Friction in creating detailed listings (typing descriptions, tags).

## 5. Unique Value Proposition (UVP) (Task 1.5)
KomuniTrade offers a **frictionless, verified, and strictly local** trading experience.
- **AI Magic:** Upload a photo, and our CNN/OCR pipeline auto-generates the title, tags, and price suggestion.
- **Trust First:** Sellers are verified via facial recognition, and a ML model calculates a credibility score.
- **Hyperlocal Focus:** Search results are strictly relevant to your immediate community using geohash indexing.

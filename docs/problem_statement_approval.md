## 1. Project Background
The Maharlika Republic platform is a hybrid digital storefront and showroom mapping system developed for **Maharlika Marexx Republic Davao** (Maharlika Gadgets) to streamline pre-owned and brand-new Apple device browsing, financing transparency, and store discovery.

To monitor and approve the development gradually, the client and developers have established the following Problem Statement Approval Matrix to track the core issues identified in the legacy process and establish how they are resolved by the system.
